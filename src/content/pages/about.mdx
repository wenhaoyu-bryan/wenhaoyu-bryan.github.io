## Short Intro

I'm Wenhao Yu, an AI product manager interested in the messy middle between business problems, product systems, and working software.

Most of my work starts with ambiguity: a vague business need, a half-formed AI idea, a workflow that people do manually, or a dataset that has value but no clear product shape yet. I like turning that kind of material into prototypes, product briefs, playbooks, and systems that other people can actually use.

For a focused visual overview, read [How I Build](/about/manifesto/).

## What I Build

- **An ontology platform for industrial settings.** I contribute to product design and research. The goal is to model and extract data from enterprise data platforms into a semantic network of entities, relationships, rules, metrics, and actions that an agent platform can call within defined permissions.
- **An enterprise agent platform.** I design around real business tasks, tool use, context, human review, and traceable execution.
- **Data and context infrastructure for enterprise AI.** I am exploring governed data access for agents; a potential data-pipeline product is still a research direction.
- **AI-assisted product discovery and prototypes.** I use AI to synthesize user research and needs, then turn scenarios into interactive product prototypes with reusable components and a frontend architecture—not just static HTML.
- Practical AI PM methods and playbooks, alongside product growth experiments.

## What I Am Currently Exploring

- **Enterprise knowledge graphs and ontology platforms:** giving data-ready organizations richer, traceable agent context without hard-coding every process in skills and workflows.
- **AI-assisted development pipelines:** exploring how vibe coding can connect user insights, scenario design, reusable prototypes, and engineering validation so teams can repeatedly build useful implementations.
- **Product-led growth (PLG):** how product experience drives activation, retention, and distribution beyond search traffic.
- **Frontier AI in enterprise settings:** following recursive self-improvement (RSI) while asking how evaluation, permissions, and human review keep pace with growing capabilities.

## Selected Background

**May–Aug 2024 — Product Growth & Development, [Leiga](https://leiga.com).** Built the SEO foundations for an AI-powered project-management SaaS — technical groundwork, metadata and structured-data systems, content architecture, and programmatic landing pages that are still live. I wrote the work up as [Experiment 00 in the Growth Lab](/growth-lab).

My background combines AI product work, industrial operations, data-driven growth, and engineering-oriented education. I have worked across industrial AI, SEO growth, product investment analysis, and B2B product contexts.
